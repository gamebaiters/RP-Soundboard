GameBaiters Soundboard v2.3.6
=============================

The honesty release. The waveform now shows what your effects actually
do to the sound instead of inventing a shape, autotune finally does
something audible, closing one channel no longer takes the others with
it, and the whole interface got a pass for space and clarity.

Waveform
--------

* FIX - The waveform drawn while effects are active was wrong. It read
  the position along the timeline as if it were a frequency, so moving
  an EQ band deformed the drawing in a way that had nothing to do with
  the audio - and the same guesswork was applied to the compressor,
  the saturator, generation loss and the rest. It now runs the REAL
  effects over the decoded audio and draws the result: what you see is
  what the chain does.
* FIX - Turning the Audio Sandbox on made the whole waveform look like
  it was clipping from end to end, even with every effect reset. It
  was a drawing bug - nothing was clipping - and it is gone.
* NEW - Effects that cannot honestly be drawn on the same timeline
  (Paulstretch, delay, the spatial stages, the modulation effects) now
  leave the waveform alone instead of faking a change.
* NEW - Boost an EQ band far enough and you SEE the wave hit the
  ceiling; switch the failsafe anti-clip on and you see it stop.

Audio sandbox
-------------

* NEW - A module button next to the chain-order reset: show or hide any
  DSP module globally, straight from the sandbox window. Same switch as
  the one in Settings, now where you actually notice you need it.
* FIX - The EQ band sliders had lost their segmented LED look and were
  drawn as plain sliders. The LEDs, the level response and the fader
  cap are back.
* FIX - Disabling Paulstretch did not survive a restart; it came back
  enabled every session.

Button options
--------------

* FIX - The per-button options window could open taller than the
  screen, leaving OK and Cancel unreachable. It scrolls now, and the
  buttons stay outside the scrolling area where you can always click
  them.
* NEW - Every section of that window folds away with one click, and it
  remembers what you left open.

Channels
--------

* FIX - Closing a channel while others were playing closed ALL the
  channels below it. Now only the one you closed goes away; the rest
  keep playing untouched.
* FIX - Temporary channels never showed their glowing border - the glow
  was being clipped away. They are now unmistakable.
* FIX - The channel row is noticeably shorter: tighter sliders and
  spacing, same controls, more room for the sound buttons.
* FIX - The microphone row now has the same frame as every other
  channel (it is a channel, after all). The oversized red "LIVE" badge
  is gone - the red border already says it.
* FIX - The Reverb label was cut off in Italian.

Main toolbar
------------

* NEW - A voice indicator next to the mute controls. It watches your
  own microphone: grey when you are silent, green when your voice is
  going out to the server, amber when you are talking but something is
  blocking it (muted during playback, or TeamSpeak not transmitting).
  Playing a sound does not light it up. Can be switched off in
  Settings.
* FIX - The mute controls are named for what they do: "Only for
  others", "Only for me", "Auto-mute my mic". The full explanation is
  in the tooltip.
* FIX - When the window gets narrow, the Rows / Cols selectors are the
  first thing to go and the mute controls are the last - they decide
  who hears what.
* NEW - A divider between the soundboard and the controls underneath,
  and the line between the buttons and the channels now sits in the
  middle of the gap instead of glued to the buttons.
* NEW - Settings > Channels > Layout: "Restore default proportions"
  puts the divider between buttons and channels back where a fresh
  install has it.

Microphone FX
-------------

* FIX - Autotune did nothing on a real voice. Its pitch detector could
  not physically reach the low notes it advertised and gave up on
  anything breathy, so the correction never engaged. It has been
  replaced with a proper detector plus a note hold, which is what makes
  the effect audible.

laundromat, a construction site or a thunderstorm; buttons can spawn

Buttons and channels

* NEW - Per-button temporary channels. Tick "Always play in a

* NEW - Per-button Audio Sandbox. A button can carry a complete

* NEW - The divider between the sound buttons and the channels is now

TeamSpeak integration

Backup
------

Interface
---------

* NEW - Interface text size setting: follow the system font (default)

* FIX - The soundboard no longer breaks under custom TeamSpeak themes.

* FIX - Pitch, speed and microphone gain sliders show their fill

* FIX - Every slider in the soundboard now shares one identical style,

* FIX - The window survives being made small: toolbar groups and panels

* FIX - Text buttons across the channel row and the Audio Sandbox were

Audio
-----

* FIX - Sounds no longer start pitched and sped up for the first

Stability
---------

* FIX - TeamSpeak no longer shows a crash dialog when you close it.
  The client was destroying a font entry that belonged to the plugin

* FIX - Temporary channels no longer break pause and stop on the other
  channels.
* FIX - macOS interface fixes: the soundboard renders with correct

GameBaiters Soundboard v2.3.4
=============================

The stability release. The crash that made a "ghost" of you on the
server is gone, macOS no longer freezes while loading links, and the
file badges + waveform animation are now fully yours to style.

Stability
---------

* FIX - TeamSpeak could crash the moment you disconnected from a server
  while the soundboard had unsaved changes. The crash also left a
  "ghost" of you on the server - a clone with your identity that kept
  timing out and reappearing in the logs. Settings are now saved right
  after the disconnect completes instead of in the middle of it.

* FIX - Disconnecting while a stream was loaded could stall TeamSpeak
  for several seconds (or crash it) while the network stream was being
  closed. The close is now aborted instantly and safely.

* FIX - macOS: loading a link could freeze the whole soundboard UI for
  up to 15 seconds (or until the stream gave up). One stuck network
  open was blocking every other channel. The UI now never waits on a
  busy worker - macOS behaves like Windows.

File badges (new)
-----------------

* NEW - Coloured format/quality badges before local file names. FLAC is
  green, MP3 orange, OGG purple, OPUS magenta, AAC red, WAV steel blue -
  and the quality pill is coloured by tier: gold for hi-res lossless,
  green for lossless, teal / amber / red for high / mid / low bitrate.

* NEW - You choose how much to show: Settings > Channels > "File badge
  before the file name" - nothing, format only, quality only, or both.

* NEW - The WEB / LIVE badge on stream titles can now be turned off too
  (Settings > Channels).

* NEW - Badge colours blend toward your custom theme's accent when a
  theme is active, so they always sit well in your palette.

Waveform animation
------------------

* FIX - The animated colour flow on the played waveform could turn
  invisible with certain custom theme colours (an accent close to the
  background made the moving tint disappear). It now guarantees
  contrast against the actual background, whatever colours you pick.

* NEW - Full animation customization in Settings > Streaming >
  Appearance: pick your own two animation colours (or leave them on
  Auto to follow the theme), and set the animation speed and intensity
  with sliders.

Streaming (YouTube / links)
---------------------------

* NEW - The loading strip now narrates every stage of a link load:
  "Starting the stream engine...", "Contacting the site...",
  "Extracting the audio track info...", then "Opening: <title>" with
  the actual video title (or "Connecting to live: <title>" for live
  broadcasts) - so you always know what it is doing and what it found.

* FIX - Faster and more reliable link loading. The streaming engine now
  keeps a persistent cache (no more re-downloading the same data on
  every link), updates itself at most once a day instead of at every
  start, and a link pasted during an engine update no longer hangs -
  the update steps aside and the link loads.

* FIX - macOS: link resolving now gets the extra time it legitimately
  needs on first use, instead of being cut off and reported as a
  failure.

Localization
------------

* NEW - Every new option and message in this release is fully
  translated in Italian.

Audio sandbox

* FIX - Enabling a Voice FX effect now turns the Voice FX module on for

* FIX - The formant shifter starts at an audible setting, so turning it

* FIX - Cleaner shimmer diffusion and a steadier dynamic EQ under load.

Streaming (YouTube / links)
---------------------------

* NEW - Seeks recover by themselves. If a stream's link dies mid-play

* FIX - Macros save streaming channels properly. A macro that captured a

* Playlist button on the channel now says "Playlist".

Auto-updater
------------

GameBaiters Soundboard v2.3.2
=============================

Patch for the streaming release.

* FIX - Link streaming now works on macOS (and Linux). TeamSpeak's

GameBaiters Soundboard v2.3.1
=============================

The streaming release. Paste a YouTube (or any http/https media) link
and it plays LIVE through the whole 14-effect DSP chain - nothing is
downloaded to disk, everything is resolved and streamed on demand.
Whole playlists, a real live voice changer for your own microphone
with shareable presets, smooth talk-over ducking, and a big pass over
the About / How-to help. Powered by a bundled, self-updating yt-dlp
that never needs any setup.

Highlights
----------

* NEW - Live link streaming (YouTube & any URL). Paste a link and it
  streams live through the full effect chain. Three ways to load one:
  paste it straight as a CHANNEL NAME (the name turns green, the video
  title shows on the waveform, the real name returns on stop); save it
  to a grid button ("Save link..." or drag a URL onto a cell); or drop
  a URL onto a channel to play at once. Normal videos get a full
  waveform with seek / reverse / crop / download, exactly like a local
  file; true live broadcasts show a purple "LIVE" notice (no seek).
  Nothing is ever left on disk - every play resolves on demand.

* NEW - Playlists. Paste or save a playlist link and choose "Whole
  playlist" or "Just this video". Whole opens a panel listing every
  track: click a track to load it, tick Autoplay to advance to the
  next automatically, reopen the panel any time from the channel's
  list button. A whole playlist can also be saved onto a grid button
  (right-click a channel that has one, or drop a playlist link on a
  button) so one click reopens the entire playlist later.

* NEW - Save the audio to a file. On a normal video the channel's
  export turns into a green "Save audio" download, and the cell
  right-click offers "Download ... audio here" straight into a button.
  Live streams are link-only.

* NEW - Microphone voice changer (Mic FX presets + sharing). The mic
  channel gains real voice presets with icon buttons - Save (floppy),
  Delete (bin), Share (copy to clipboard) and Paste (apply a shared
  one) - plus "Save Mic FX package into macro" so one button toggles a
  whole voice on and off.

* NEW - Voice & transmission (Settings). "Use voice activation while a
  sound plays" sends the soundboard continuously but only transmits
  YOUR voice when you actually speak. "Lower the soundboard when I
  talk" ducks the soundboard SMOOTHLY while you speak and lifts it
  back when you stop, with an adjustable dip amount.

* NEW - Settings > Streaming. Pick the audio quality (Best / Balanced
  / Data saver) and toggle "Auto-play files loaded from a link" (off
  by default: a link loads paused and ready).

* NEW - Self-managing streaming engine. yt-dlp is bundled per platform,
  hidden (no console flashes), and silently auto-updated on every
  startup. The About dialog shows its version with its own "Update
  streaming engine" button and attribution; a start/close cleaner
  guarantees no temp files or ghost processes are ever left behind.

* NEW - How-to & About refresh. The in-app guide gains full sections on
  live streaming / playlists and the microphone voice changer (with
  emoji signposting), fully translated to Italian. The About streaming
  blurb was trimmed and the FFmpeg attribution corrected.

Streaming reliability
---------------------

* Forward-seek on a YouTube VOD now lands where you click. The seek was
  reworked to a format-level seek (what "ffmpeg -ss" does) and, for the
  odd file whose byte-range seek the CDN rejects, the decoder reopens at
  the start and sequentially fast-forwards to the target - so the seek
  always lands, instead of the channel silently vanishing.

* Network stalls are survived, not fatal. A brief "buffering" strip
  shows while a dropped connection reconnects; only a genuinely dead
  URL gives up, and then it restarts from the beginning and shows a
  transient "network error" notice rather than disappearing.

* Seek spam no longer freezes TeamSpeak. Rapid waveform clicks collapse
  to the LAST target (earlier loads are dropped); the network open runs
  off the GUI thread so the whole client stays responsive.

* No self-inflicted throttling. Recovery reopens are paced so a half-
  dead CDN node is not hammered with range requests (which used to make
  googlevideo throttle the whole session).

Fixes
-----

* Playlist panel no longer vanishes the instant it opens (a phantom
  rename event was unloading it); pasting a second playlist link now
  replaces the first instead of re-showing the old one.

* Autoplay now respects order - clicking the first track no longer
  skips straight to the third (each load's stop event was advancing the
  cursor).

* Loading strip and playlist / cancel buttons render correctly (no more
  mismatched background colour or garbled glyphs).

* Closing the vinyl / scratch popup after skipping no longer jumps the
  audio forward a few seconds before it closes.

* Clearing a channel name to empty restores the default "Channel N"
  name instead of leaving it blank.

* About dialog no longer shows stray braces after the FFmpeg line (a
  corrupted Italian translation string).


GameBaiters Soundboard v2.3.0
=============================

The largest feature release since the fork. A full live microphone
FX chain with a dedicated pinned channel and 13 ready-made voice
presets, a ten-effect "Voice FX" macro module, a real DJ vinyl /
turntable scratch engine per channel, convolution reverb, binaural
beats, a psychoacoustic bass enhancer, a per-channel LFO modulation
matrix, an FX automation recorder, EBU-R128 loudness normalisation,
2x-oversampled saturation and a true-peak limiter. Plus a Settings
pass that groups every option into clear sub-categories, and a long
audio-quality campaign that makes the vinyl scratch seamless in both
directions while keeping live FX real-time.

Highlights
----------

* NEW - Microphone FX. A dedicated pinned "Microphone" channel at
  the top of the channel list (reduced UI: no waveform / transport)
  processes your live voice through a curated DSP chain in real
  time. Toggle it from the button next to "+ Channel"; the whole
  feature can be switched off in Settings (every related control
  then disappears). Live 1:1 pitch shift (granular, no time
  stretch), an always-on output limiter, optional self-monitor
  ("hear my own processed voice", default off), a TS3 "Toggle Mic
  FX" hotkey, and full state persistence. Auto-bypasses on any DSP
  error so the mic is never left dead.

* NEW - 13 voice presets. Robot, Chipmunk, Demon, Telephone,
  Military radio, Alien, Cave, Underwater, Megaphone, Helium,
  Giant, Ghost and Vintage AM radio - one click each, built on the
  mic chain + Voice FX macro.

* NEW DSP - Voice FX (one macro stage, ten sub-effects). Autotune,
  Vocoder (16-band), Ring Modulator, Tremolo, Vibrato, Auto-Wah,
  Formant shift, Shimmer, Reverse-Reverb (grain-reverse delay) and
  Exciter, each independently togglable and settable inside one
  collapsible accordion. Shares a granular pitch shifter with
  Autotune / Shimmer / the mic live pitch.

* NEW - Vinyl / Tape-Stop scratch (per channel). A transport-row
  button opens a popup with an animated spinning disc. Grab and
  drag the disc to SCRATCH the audio like a real turntable - both
  directions, position-locked to your hand. HOLD = brake (rate
  ramps 1 -> 0 over an adjustable time), release = spin back up,
  single click = one-shot full stop. Backward scratch is
  effectively infinite (a background decoder back-fills older audio
  on demand) while memory stays a fixed 10 s ring - scratch back
  through a ten-hour file without a huge buffer. The mouse pointer
  vanishes "into the disc" during the drag (FPS-style capture) and
  returns on release. Vinyl button visibility toggles in Settings.

* NEW DSP - Convolution reverb. The Reverb module gains an
  Algorithmic / Convolution mode combo. Partitioned FFT (OLA)
  convolution with four synthesised impulse responses (Hall,
  Church, Room, Spring - each with its own distinct signature) plus
  "Load IR..." to convolve against any user WAV (up to 3 s). Hybrid-
  normalised so presets are audible and distinct without distorting.
  Default stays Algorithmic (optional feature).

* NEW DSP - Bass Enhancer. Psychoacoustic low-end restoration:
  synthesises harmonics of the fundamental so small speakers imply
  bass they cannot physically reproduce.

* NEW DSP - Binaural beats. Adds a detuned sine pair (L/R): base
  frequency 100-500 Hz, beat 0.5-40 Hz, level in dB.

* NEW - LFO modulation matrix (per channel). Two LFOs (rate
  0.05-20 Hz, sine / triangle / square / sample-and-hold, depth)
  routed through four assignable rows to a curated set of DSP
  targets (ring-mod frequency, auto-wah centre, reverb mix, chorus
  depth, saturator drive, bitcrusher rate, volume, pan, vibrato
  depth...). Applied per block ahead of the stage chain.

* NEW - FX automation recorder. Record captures timestamped
  sandbox-state snapshots (~30 Hz) during playback; Play replays
  them through the normal state path; Loop repeats; save / load to
  a JSON file.

* NEW - Sandbox module manager. Settings > "Audio Sandbox Modules"
  gives every one of the 21 DSP stages its own checkbox. Unchecked
  = the stage is hidden from the sandbox UI (accordion + pipeline
  block) AND globally bypassed on the audio thread.

* NEW - Microphone reverb slider + decimal pitch. The mic FX panel
  gets its own reverb wet slider, and its pitch control now steps in
  0.1-semitone increments.

* Settings reorganised. The Channels and General pages group their
  (now many) options into labelled sub-categories instead of a flat
  wall of checkboxes.

Audio quality
-------------

* EBU-R128 loudness normalisation (Q2). Optional (Settings, default
  off): every sound opens with a single-pass loudnorm to -16 LUFS so
  a playlist of mixed-loudness clips plays at an even level. Skipped
  in reverse mode (the per-chunk graph rebuild would restart its
  convergence).

* 2x-oversampled saturation (Q1). The nonlinear shapers run at double
  rate (halfband up / down) to keep aliasing out of hot drive
  settings. Toggle: "HQ oversampling" (default on).

* True-peak limiter (Q3). The limiter sidechain estimates inter-
  sample peaks via 4x interpolation so masters no longer clip a D/A
  reconstruction that the sample-peak meter never saw.

Seamless vinyl - the scratch campaign
-------------------------------------

* Backward scratch is seamless and effectively infinite. A dedicated
  off-thread back-fill decoder feeds older audio into the ring's
  oldest end (matched to the channel's live pitch + reverb), so
  scratching back never runs dry and never allocates unbounded
  memory.

* Release "re-homes" to the needle. Letting go after a big backward
  scratch resumes forward playback from exactly where you scratched
  to (real-turntable behaviour) instead of snapping back to the pre-
  scratch position - the flywheel spins up on the ring while an async
  prime-seek re-points the decoder, with no dropout.

* Real-time FX while the vinyl is open. The transparent armed decode-
  ahead is kept tiny so live pitch / speed / reverb / every effect
  stays responsive; the full forward-scratch runway is built only
  during an active gesture.

* Fast-forward is smooth and never jumps backward. The forward scrub-
  seek rebuilds its runway fast, keeps the effect tails alive across
  each needle-jump (no more FX cutting out and ramping back), and re-
  anchors the cursor so a fast forward stroke can no longer snap the
  position backward.

GameBaiters Soundboard v2.2.17
==============================

Massive feature + audio-quality release on top of v2.2.16. Four
new DSP modules (Noise Gate, De-esser, Transient Shaper, Dynamic
EQ), physical Doppler on the Leia rotating modes, cross-slot
sidechain routing for every dynamics stage, first spectrogram
view of the channel waveform, per-channel vertical LED meter,
multi-channel "infinity" mode that spawns temporary channels on
demand, six new user-facing options in Settings, and one more
round of frying fixes tightening the Leia HRTF crossfade under
rotation. Full pipeline colour update, complete IT translation
for every new string, persistence pass so every option survives
the restart.

Highlights
----------

* NEW DSP - Noise Gate. Full-band gate with independent attack /
  hold / release, 3 dB hysteresis, and RANGE floor (max -80 dB).
  Sidechain source selector: -1 = self, 0..31 = read another
  slot's envelope via the new cross-slot sidechain bus. Classic
  post-mic clean-up + creative gating on either the same channel
  or a partner track.

* NEW DSP - De-esser. Sibilance-band compressor. Narrow bandpass
  biquad isolates the trigger region (default 6.5 kHz, Q=3.0);
  envelope crossing threshold pulls the FULL signal down by up to
  RANGE dB. Cross-slot sidechain lets the de-ess of one channel
  react to the HF energy of another (podcast + noise-bed pair).

* NEW DSP - Transient Shaper. Dual envelope follower (fast +
  slow); the ratio isolates attack vs sustain and each is scaled
  independently by +/-20 dB. Positive attack = punchier hits;
  negative attack = smoothed. Positive sustain = fuller body;
  negative sustain = tighter release.

* NEW DSP - Dynamic EQ (4 bands). Each band is a peaking biquad
  driven by an envelope on a filtered copy of the input. Static
  gain = baseline EQ shape; Dynamic gain kicks in above threshold
  with the given ratio (negative = downward compression on that

    1. HRIR crossfade window bumped 15 ms -> 30 ms. Longer fade
       flattens the per-sample crossfade envelope slope and lets
       the pending chain settle more of its own OLA tail into
       the output while the fade is still in progress.

       smooth in the SOFA grid) and the crossfade replaces the
       copied tail with the new IR's own tail across the window
       instead of ADDING one tail to zero.

Crop-marker persistence rebuild on top of v2.2.15. Closes the

Highlights
----------

* STALE-STOP GUARD — the real root cause. onStopPlaying is a
  Qt::QueuedConnection signal, so when the user clicks cell B
  while cell A is playing on the same slot, ordering is:
    1. buttonTriggered runs: stopPlayback(slot) queues stop A.
    2. buttonTriggered runs: playSoundInSlot(slot, B) queues
       start B AND, inside Sampler, also queues a second stop
       for the displaced A.

       branch calls s_slotToBtnIdx.remove(slot), WIPING the
       freshly installed B_idx mapping.
    5. start B handler reads s_slotToBtnIdx.value(slot, -1)
       and sees -1.
  From that moment on, every right-click crop edit on the
  slot falls into applyCropEdit's btn-not-found branch and the
  save is silently skipped. Reproducer (user-reported):

    still plays -> click A back -> markers reverted.
  Fix: at onStopPlaying handler entry, check the sampler's
  current state for the slot. If it reports ePLAYING or ePAUSED,
  a fresh playback owns the slot now — drop any stale
  s_pendingHardClear flag and return without touching the
  channel state. Stop event was for the displaced sound; the
  new sound's mapping must stay intact. Applies to the X-click

  trigger).

  event fires. All ~15 setSoundInfo callsites in config_qt.cpp,
  main_page_wiring.cpp, and the advanced/sound-settings
  dialogs now persist on commit. Slider perf untouched.

* applyCropEdit rewritten as an EXPLICIT (newStart, newEnd)
  function (carried over from v2.2.16-pre). The previous
  mutator-lambda API conflated reading the current state,
  applying the user's intent, and deciding what to save —
  when the seed step pulled from the live slot (which gets
  wiped to defaults the moment the slot drops to eSILENT),
  the save step would write start=0, end=-1 back even when
  the user had only asked to remove ONE edge. Each waveform
  context-menu action now computes its absolute target range

  at every persist site so any future regression is

GameBaiters Soundboard v2.2.15
==============================

Targeted bug-fix release on top of v2.2.14. Closes the "reverse mode
loops a 30-200 Hz fragment of audio forever after rapid pitch / speed
dragging on a short file" bug end-to-end, and fixes waveform crop
markers losing the untouched edge on every edit.

Highlights
----------

* Reverse-mode infinite glitch loop killed at the root. Two
  cascading bugs in samples.cpp + inputfileffmpeg.cpp combined into
  a vicious cycle that, on short audio with loop=ON, pitch/speed
  dragged in reverse mode, would dribble the last tens of ms of
  audio at the file-start boundary at 30-200 Hz, sometimes for
  several seconds, then block all subsequent audio until plugin
  reload. Fixed at three independent layers:

  1. samples.cpp loop branch was checking `slot.channelReverse` to
     decide whether to seek the loop restart to cropEnd (reverse
     audio direction) or cropStart (forward). Sounds played reverse
     via the cell-level SoundInfo::reverse flag (cell context menu)
     carry audioReverse=true but channelReverse=false; the wrong
     check sent their loop restart to cropStart=0, which is already
     at minF for the reverse chunk worker, so done() flipped back to
     true on the first iteration and the slot ping-ponged forever.
     Changed to gate on slot.audioReverse so both reverse paths
     (channel button + cell flag) loop correctly.

  2. inputfileffmpeg.cpp chunk worker added a session-emitted gate
     for the cursor<=minF + empty-decode natural-end path. A cold
     filter graph immediately after a pitch/speed rebuild (or a
     loop restart) can emit one empty decode at file start before
     warming up. The old code flipped m_chunkFileEnded=true on that
     single empty, samples.cpp's done() returned true on the first
     audio-thread poll, loop branch re-seeked to cropEnd, the
     rebuild fired again, empty again, and the cycle spun forever
     with the user hearing the last fragment of audio glitching.
     Natural-end now fires only when audio HAS been emitted this
     session; cold-graph empties retry the same cursor instead.

  3. inputfileffmpeg.cpp chunk worker now tracks cursor descent rate
     between consecutive successful chunks. If the codec dribbles
     micro-slivers (< 50 ms descent per chunk) either AT the
     boundary (fast path, fires on the first sliver) or for two
     consecutive iterations anywhere else (slow path, ~500 ms
     ceiling), the worker accepts the current chunk so the listener
     hears the last legitimate slice of audio, then SNAPS the
     cursor down to minF so the next iteration cleanly hits the
     genuine natural-end path. Replaces the previous behaviour of
     silently re-decoding the same boundary chunk indefinitely.

* samples.cpp loop-restart rate guard. Belt-and-braces second line
  of defence against any other path that could spin a tight loop
  cycle (codec bug, malformed container, future regression). If
  more than 35 loop restarts fire inside a 1 s window on a slot,
  the slot is killed cleanly and the user hears silence + sees the
  reload glyph (in replay mode) instead of a glitch storm. Normal
  loop usage of a 200 ms file at neutral speed = 5 loops / s; even
  extreme legit usage (100 ms file at speed=3) = 30 loops / s,
  both well below the threshold. The guard is reset on every fresh
  playSoundInSlot so a previous-session burst can't carry over.

* Waveform crop markers now persist correctly across every edit.
  Two distinct bugs:

  1. applyCropEdit was seeding both edges from the live playing
     slot via Sampler::getSlotCrop, which returns the 0.0 / -1.0
     defaults when the slot is eSILENT. Stopped slots therefore
     ran every right-click edit against the defaults, then wrote
     BOTH cropStartValue and cropStopValue back to the model after
     a single-edge mutate — wiping whichever edge the user did not
     touch. User-reported symptom: "modifying or removing a marker
     wipes the other too, the change is only temporary anyway".
     Fixed by preferring live-slot crop only when the slot actually
     has one, otherwise seeding from the cell's stored SoundInfo
     (getStartTime + getPlayTime → absolute end second).

  2. The save block wrote `cropStartValue = anyCrop ? round(sCur)
     : 0`, which dropped the start to 0 whenever the user set ONLY
     the end (because sCur was 0 + eCur > 0 still flagged anyCrop).
     Per-field condition split so each edge persists independently:
     `cropStartValue = (sCur > 0) ? round(sCur) : 0`, same for end.

  3. Crop edits now call writeConfigImmediate after setSoundInfo so
     the change reaches disk on the right-click commit instead of
     waiting for the next play / window-close / disconnect flush.
     Matches user expectation: markers set today are still set on
     next session restart even if no other state-changing event
     fires in between.

GameBaiters Soundboard v2.2.11

UX-focused release on top of v2.2.10. Reworks the channel transport

Channel transport: replay-from-cursor
-------------------------------------

- Audio finishing naturally or being stopped via the red stop button

EQ spectrum meter — full recalibration
--------------------------------------

- The per-band level analyser is fed POST-chain and POST-volume now,
  so the LEDs reflect exactly what the listener hears. EQ boosts,
  every effect in the chain, mono fold, soft-limit, the slot's
  volume slider, and the earrape-protection intensity all colour the
  LEDs honestly.
- Scale switched from linear /160 (where loud bass was the only
  thing that ever visibly moved) to a pro-audio dB window of
  -48..+3 dB. Quiet content (~-30 dBFS) renders at 47 % instead of
  3 %; 0 dBFS / clipping content saturates the strip cleanly.
- Full-scale reference recalibrated to kFftSize/8 to compensate for
  practical losses: softLimit caps time-domain peaks around
  -0.5 dB, distorted/squarewave content spreads its fundamental
  across multiple bins so the peak bin lands around -3 to -6 dB. The
  old kFftSize/4 reference left the meter pinned 6 cells below the
  top even on real earrape; +6 dB of calibration brings clipped
  material all the way up.
- Above-thumb cells (the "cut" zone above the slider knob) keep
  responding to audio level but at half the saturation of the
  below-thumb zone, with a darker ghost overlay on the rising
  meter column. The spectrum stays readable even on heavily cut
  bands - "the cursor is below this cell but the spectrum is here".

EQ band coverage — frequency-adaptive Q
---------------------------------------

- The 2/3-octave default Q (2.145) gave the low five bands a
  bandwidth so narrow that the 20 Hz slider only affected
  ~14-26 Hz, a range below the content of nearly every real audio
  file. Bands 0..4 (20, 25, 40, 63, 100 Hz) now use Q = 1.4,
  roughly doubling their bandwidth so the slider actually catches
  the bulk of real bass content. The mid + high register stays at
  the original 2.145 for precision.
- A first attempt at Q = 0.7 on the same bands was reverted: five
  adjacent low bands at that Q overlapped so heavily that a smile-
  curve preset (+12 dB on each) summed to +20+ dB in the 30..80 Hz

  presets musical while still making the low sliders feel alive.

Bug fixes

  / reverse / sync, and start with an empty filename label / wave-

GameBaiters Soundboard v2.2.10
==============================

Spatial-engine reshuffle, EQ meter overhaul, reverse-seek dead-worker
fix, and a zero-warning clean Windows build on top of v2.2.9.

Spatial engine
--------------

- Leia (measured-HRTF convolution) is now the default 3D engine for
  every new channel. The legacy Classic parametric engine is kept
  available as a Settings → Channels preference for users who prefer
  its lightweight character — the per-channel selector was removed

- 8D preset now actually starts from the FRONT azimuth instead of one

  rotBlockCounter on both audio paths, runs the standard reset
  (filter delays / ring buffers), and restores rotation so the source
  continues from where it was when the user flipped direction.

EQ meter
--------

- Per-band LED segment count tripled from 16 → 48 so high-frequency
  bands (4 k / 8 k / 16 k) that rarely peak now have fine-grained
  visual resolution and you can actually see them respond.
- Band-level computation rewritten from MEAN to PEAK magnitude
  across the 2/3-octave window. Mean across 150+ FFT bins for a high
  band (16 k) gave ~1 % meter response on a full-scale sine because
  the signal energy in 1-3 bins was averaged across the whole window.
  Peak keeps band-shape sharp regardless of bin count and lets even
  loud broadband material light the strip to the top.
- Headroom recalibrated: full-scale sine through the Hann window now
  hits ~1.0 (saturation), realistic loud material (-6 dBFS RMS, peak
  near 0 dBFS) reaches the top of the strip cleanly. Was previously
  capped around ~30 % even on extreme earrape.

Reverse playback
----------------

- Repeatedly clicking around on the waveform during reverse playback
  no longer eventually kills the audio. The chunk worker branches at
  cursor<=minF AND at consecutive-empty-cap now PARK on the condvar
  with restart-pending instead of breaking out — seek can revive the
  worker. The previous design left the worker dead the moment any
  one seek landed near minF, queue then stayed empty,
  producer-thread saw done flag → readSamples flipped m_done = true
  → slot terminated even though the user wanted to keep playing.
- seek() in streaming-reverse mode now clears m_chunkWorkerDone in
  addition to wiping the queue, so a previously-parked worker is
  woken up on the next click + the audio pipeline doesn't latch
  m_done = true between the seek and the queue refill.

Warning-free build
------------------

- AVCodec::sample_fmts + AVCodec::supported_samplerates migrated to
  avcodec_get_supported_config(AV_CODEC_CONFIG_SAMPLE_FORMAT /
  SAMPLE_RATE). New API gated on
  LIBAVCODEC_VERSION_INT >= AV_VERSION_INT(61, 13, 100) so FFmpeg
  7.1+ (Windows static, macOS Homebrew) uses it; FFmpeg 6.1 (Linux
  ubuntu-24.04 system) falls through to the deprecated fields path.
- MSVC: _CRT_SECURE_NO_WARNINGS + _CRT_NONSTDC_NO_WARNINGS to mute
  the dozen-or-so POSIX-CRT-name C4996 noises (fopen / sprintf /

  on internal-only data, so the Windows-secure variants would add
  noise without security benefit.
- MSVC: /wd4267 (size_t -> int truncation in SampleBuffer.h —

  redefinition).
- /DELAYLOAD pragmas in plugin.cpp removed — they pointed at FFmpeg
  DLLs that don't exist in the static build, emitting LNK4229 on
  every link. LoadLibraryEx fallback kept for the case where the
  build flavour ever switches back to shared FFmpeg.
- /NODEFAULTLIB:libcmt added so the FFmpeg static archives' /MT CRT
  reference doesn't fight the plugin's /MD CRT (LNK4098).
- Net result: zero plugin warnings on a clean Windows build.


GameBaiters Soundboard v2.2.9
=============================

Reverse-playback overhaul + bundled FFmpeg bump (n6.1.1 → n8.1.1) on top
of v2.2.8.

Reverse-playback overhaul
-------------------------

- Streaming-reverse decoder. The old whole-file pre-decode pass
  (m_reverseBuf) is replaced by a chunk worker that decodes the file
  backward in 3-second chunks anchored at the cursor. Memory cap per
  slot is ~1.7 MB regardless of source length (was: whole file, ~57 MB
  for a 5-minute stereo MP3).
- Cursor starts at file end on reverse-from-open. Was seeded to
  cropStart and the rate-limiter pinned the displayed cursor at the
  start position while audio played backward — fixed by seeding from
  the decoder (which open() sets to cropEnd / file length on the
  reverse path).
- New audioReverse slot flag mirrors `sound.reverse ||
  channelReverse`. Cursor branch and rate-limit guard read this
  instead of channelReverse-only, so per-CELL reverse cells now also
  hit the reverse cursor path (previously fell into the forward
  branch + monotonic-up clamp → cursor pinned at start).
- Larger chunks for cleaner seams. CHUNK_SEC bumped 0.5 s → 3.0 s.
  atempo filter state is reset on every chunk seek, so previously
  there was a seam every 500 ms — now one per 3 s. Worker decode
  budget (~80-200 ms per chunk) is 15-30× safer relative to chunk
  runtime, so the queue never drains under playback.
- Queue cap MAX_QUEUE_CHUNKS 8 → 3. Same total buffer (~9 s) with
  fewer transient allocations.

FFmpeg n6.1.1 → n8.1.1
----------------------

- Static FFmpeg upgraded to 8.1.1 'Hoare' (5 May 2026) on Windows.
  ABI bump: avutil 58→60, avcodec 60→62, avformat 60→62, avfilter
  9→11, swresample 4→6. DLL names + uninstall list updated.
- All ch_layout / AVChannelLayout / swr_alloc_set_opts2 API paths
  already gated on LIBAVCODEC_VERSION_INT >= 59.37.100 from the v2.2.7
  encoder rewrite — the legacy `channels` / `channel_layout` fields
  branch is now dead code in every shipping configuration.
- Cache key bumped to ffmpeg-static-msvc-x64-n8.1.1-v6-ffmpeg8 so the
  GitHub Actions cache + local .recipe-version both force a clean
  rebuild on first run.
- Linux CI still uses ubuntu-24.04's system FFmpeg 6.1 (avcodec 60
  also satisfies the gate). macOS uses Homebrew (auto-tracks latest
  8.x). No source changes needed for either.

Instant reverse-toggle release on top of v2.2.8. The Reverse button on
the waveform player no longer freezes TeamSpeak while the file is
decoded — the heavy pre-decode + filter-graph rebuild moved off the
audio mutex and the GUI thread onto a per-slot background worker, so
clicking Reverse mid-playback now returns immediately and the
direction flips as soon as the new buffer is ready (sub-second for
short SFX, a few seconds for multi-minute music, but in either case
WITHOUT a frozen client).

What changed under the hood
---------------------------

- Async reverse worker
  setSlotReverse now snapshots the slot state under a microsecond
  lock, hands the full close / re-open / pre-decode / re-reverse pass
  to a std::thread per slot, and returns. The OLD inputFile keeps
  streaming through the producer thread for the entire decode window
  — audio never goes silent and the GUI cursor never stops moving.
- Micro-lock swap
  When the worker finishes, it disconnects the producer thread from
  the old file OUTSIDE the audio lock, then acquires the Sampler
  mutex only for the pointer shuffle + buffer drain (microseconds
  instead of seconds). The old InputFile is closed + freed AFTER the
  lock is released so the audio thread is held up just long enough
  to publish the new pointer.
- Cooperative cancellation
  Rapid Reverse-button mashing no longer queues up multi-second
  decode jobs. A shared_ptr<atomic<bool>> cancel token per worker is
  polled inside the FFmpeg pre-decode loop every 64 packets, so a
  superseded worker aborts within ~10-100 ms. The same token fires
  on Stop and on plugin shutdown so a long file never wedges TS3
  during teardown.
- Faster pre-decode itself
  m_reverseBuf now pre-reserves the full expected sample count from
  the container header (one allocation instead of ~24 amortised
  reallocs on a 5-minute file). The in-place buffer reverse uses a
  32-bit swap on stereo material, halving the swap-loop instruction
  count.
- Epoch-checked safety nets
  stopSlotInternal and Sampler::shutdown both bump the worker epoch
  + set the cancel flag, so an in-flight worker that has already
  passed the cancel checkpoint sees the epoch mismatch under the
  final lock and quietly throws away its new InputFile instead of
  swapping it into a slot that no longer wants it.

What you experience
-------------------

- Click Reverse on a 10-second SFX: indistinguishable from instant
  (decode is a few ms, swap is microseconds, audio just flips).
- Click Reverse on a 5-minute song: TS3 stays fully responsive while
  the new buffer builds in the background; you hear the song
  continue forward for a moment, then the direction flips when the
  buffer is ready (no freeze, no audio gap on the swap).
- Click Reverse twice quickly: the first worker is cancelled within
  ~50 ms, the second one takes over without queueing the first
  decode to completion.
- Stop a long reverse playback that is still pre-decoding: the
  decode aborts within ~50 ms instead of running to completion in
  the background.

Compatibility
-------------

- No save format / preset / settings file changes. Existing INIs and
  sandbox presets load unchanged.
- The reverse output is bit-identical to v2.2.8 for the same source +
  pitch / speed settings — only the build path moved threads.

  pitch or speed was modified. The cursor's pixel position is
  computed against the full file length; with a crop active the
  marker overlay covers the trimmed edges, but the cursor line
  itself was drawn unclipped, so it tracked past the visible end.
  The cursor and the played-portion tint are now clamped to the
  [cropStart, cropEnd] pixel range that the marker overlay defines.
* Fixed: cursor jumped forward / backward while the user dragged
  the speed slider during playback. Live speed changes recompute
  bufferedSec with the new speedFactor against samples that were
  produced at the OLD factor; the raw position therefore jumped
  on every tick. The cache refresh is now monotonic (rejects
  backward jumps from this artefact) and rate-limited to a
  comfortably-above-real-time forward advance per audio cycle.
  Explicit seeks / play / loop restarts invalidate the anchor so
  legitimate position jumps (user-driven backward seek, loop wrap)
  still snap immediately.
* Fixed: with loop + non-1.0 speed + a crop range, the cursor
  reached cropEnd and froze while the audio kept playing past it.
  InputFileFFmpeg::setMaxPlayTime stored its bound in OUTPUT
  samples but the input-to-output sample ratio depends on
  speedFactor, so a 10-second crop at speed 2 actually decoded
  20 seconds of input. The bound is now stored in INPUT-file
  seconds and compared against m_filePosition (which is also in
  input-time), so the decoder stops at the requested input second
  no matter how pitch / speed change during playback. Cursor,
  audio, loop restart, and time label now all agree on where the
  end is.
* Fixed: the waveform time label read elapsed seconds against the
  full file length (e.g. "12 s / 30 s") instead of against the
  active crop range. With a crop set it now reads
  "(pos - cropStart) / (cropEnd - cropStart)", matching what the
  cursor visually expresses and what the audio actually plays.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
EXPORT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
* New: multi-format export. The Export-audio dialog now offers
  WAV (PCM 16-bit), FLAC (lossless), OGG Vorbis, and AAC in M4A.
  Format is detected from the filename extension (or the
  selected filter). Unknown extensions fall back to WAV. The
  encoder is a thin FFmpeg-backed bridge that handles channel-
  layout / sample-format conversion via swresample so every
  format receives the same DSP-chain-applied stereo PCM stream.
* Fixed: the export progress dialog was broken - the bar stuck
  at 0% on Windows native style, the chunk fill leaked outside
  the rounded background, and the final 100% green / red state
  did not always render. The dialog now uses a custom-painted
  bar (rounded track, fill clipped to a rounded path, 60 Hz
  easing toward target) instead of QProgressBar + QSS chunks,
  so the visual is reliable across styles.
* The FFmpeg static recipe (Windows) and CI cache key both
  picked up new encoders / muxers (pcm_s16le, flac, vorbis,
  aac and wav, flac, ogg, ipod, mp4 muxers). build_local.ps1
  now writes a .recipe-version marker after each FFmpeg build
  and force-rebuilds when the recipe id changes, so a stale
  cached tree never silently misses new symbols.
* The Linux runner was bumped from ubuntu-22.04 to ubuntu-24.04
  so the system FFmpeg covers the encoder APIs the bridge
  needs (swr_alloc_set_opts2, av_channel_layout_default,
  AVFrame::ch_layout - all added in FFmpeg 5.1).

GameBaiters Soundboard v2.2.6
=============================

Performance and audio-quality release on top of v2.2.5. Eliminates
GUI lag at idle and during slider drags, fixes the residual frying
buzz on the Leia engine for complex audio, restores the strength of
the Classic 8D rotating engine, and reworks the ini-save policy so
settings are persisted on user events instead of a background timer
that itself caused recurring lag. New: the audio sandbox remembers
the chosen movement engine across sessions.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PERFORMANCE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
* Fixed: system-wide GUI lag when the soundboard was loaded.
  Position polling now runs at 30 Hz instead of 60 Hz, skips
  silent slots via a lock-free atomic, and reads its data from a
  lock-free position cache updated by the audio thread - no
  mutex contention against the audio block.
* Fixed: any slider in the UI lagging behind the cursor while
  dragging. Every model setter used to perform a synchronous
  full-ini disk write on each valueChanged tick. Saves are now
  event-driven (see PERSISTENCE below).
* Fixed: 1-second audio + GUI freeze the first time the user
  picked the Leia engine. The SOFA file load + FFT plan build +
  noise probe calibration ran inside the audio mutex; it now
  runs outside the lock so audio keeps flowing through the
  Classic fallback while Leia initialises.
* Fixed: the audio thread holding m_mutex through fetchSamples
  at idle. Both audio paths now bail lock-free when no slot is
  playing, so the GUI sees the mutex completely uncontended
  with nothing happening.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
SPATIAL AUDIO
---------------------------------------------------------------------
* Fixed: residual frying buzz on the Leia engine with complex
  (broadband, high-peak) material like full songs. Three causes
  addressed together:
  - HRIR lookups widened from 0.5 deg to 3 deg deadband (well
    above the SOFA grid) so the audio thread no longer pays a
    full IR-FFT per block under rotation.
  - The live HRIR is now smoothed toward the latest lookup at
    a much slower rate (~270 ms to 95% vs the previous 27 ms).
    Each block's IR differs from the previous by a tiny amount
    so the OLA overlap adds nearly-coherently with the new
    convolution - the per-block click train that summed to a
    ~187 Hz tone is now well below the audible floor.
  - The post-Leia limiter no longer pumps on every rotational
    peak. Its ceiling was raised to 1.4 (SlotDsp's outer soft
    limiter at 1.0 still catches real overshoots), so the
    rotation-rate amplitude modulation that read as frying on
    complex material is gone.
* Fixed: rotating Leia sources also showed a finer "buzz" from
  the early-reflections engine. ShoeboxRoom now uses a per-
  sample fractional delay-line read that lerps the delay from
  the previous block to the new one across the block, instead
  of jumping the read offset stepwise every block (which was a
  zipper noise riding the reflection signal at block rate).
* Fixed: the Classic parametric 8D engine became significantly
  quieter after the v2.2.4 "boombox bass bump" fix. Rotating
  modes (3D Rotate, 8D preset) now use a wider-than-unity
  width compensation curve (1.4 at width 0 tapering to 1.0 at
  >=90 deg) - actually louder than the original pre-fix
  loudness, without the bass bump returning. 3D Manual keeps
  the original 0.5..1.0 curve since the bass bump only
  appeared on static, narrow-width sources.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PERSISTENCE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
* Reworked: ini saves are now event-triggered rather than
  running on a background timer. The model is marked dirty
  on each setter call and the disk write fires from concrete
  user events:
  - Start of playback (sound, hotkey, preview)
  - Closing the soundboard window (Mainpage or legacy)
  - Hiding the soundboard window (TS3 tab switch)
  - Switching between the 4 soundboard profiles
  - Server disconnect
  - Plugin shutdown (TeamSpeak quit, plugin unload)
* Closing the soundboard window without quitting TS3 does NOT
  reset state - it flushes the in-memory model so the next
  open finds the most recent settings.
* The previous 250 ms auto-debouncer was itself a recurring
  lag source on slider drags (one disk write a few hundred
  milliseconds after every drag). The new policy issues at
  most one write per concrete user event.
* The model's destructor and an explicit "specific path"
  branch ensure no save is silently dropped on plugin teardown.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
SANDBOX
---------------------------------------------------------------------
* New: the audio sandbox now remembers the chosen movement
  engine (Classic / Leia) across sessions. Picking an engine
  in any channel's sandbox dialog persists the choice to a
  global QSettings key; freshly-added channels default to that
  engine instead of falling back to Classic. Per-channel
  persistence (when enabled in Settings) still overrides this
  on restore for that specific channel.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
METER
---------------------------------------------------------------------
* Fixed: the peak-hold marker on the channel meter no longer
  freezes mid-fall when a sound stops. The v2.2.5 silent-slot
  optimisation drained the meter exactly once on the falling
  edge instead of letting the marker animate back to floor.
  The 25 Hz meter timer now pumps every tick; the meter's own
  change-detect kills paints once the marker reaches rest.

      ambience tuned to match the audio_sandbox standalone reference
* Brown-Duda structural HRTF, dual virtual-speaker rendering. ITD
  via Woodworth (up to 656 us), 1p/1z head shadow, 5-event sparse
  pinna FIR, shoulder + torso single-tap reflections, peaking notch
  for front/back, concha boost at 3 kHz, head-sway LFO, distance
  attenuation + air absorption shelf.
* "Spatial mix" slider blends the HRTF output with the raw stereo
  input - matches the audio_sandbox standalone reference and stops
  the source from feeling mono / over-coloured.
* Room "Ambience" reverb (Schroeder-Moorer / Freeverb topology, 8
  combs + 4 allpasses per channel). Independent from the FxPanel
  reverb - this one shapes SPACE, FxPanel shapes the SOUND.
* 16-band ISO 2/3-octave graphic EQ (-12..+12 dB, Q matched to band
  spacing, anti-denormal protection, master "Enable EQ" toggle, big
  step changes reset filter state to kill cascading transients).
* Paulstretch (Paul Nasca's phase-randomisation extreme time-stretch).
  Streaming-adapted: feeds from the live decoder ring at decode rate
  while the synth stage advances slowly via FFT phase scrambling.
  Per-path (capture + playback) instances so the stretched output
  reaches BOTH the local listener and the server, in sync with each
  channel's seek / skip / stop / restart events. Adjustable factor
  1x..50x and window 50..1000 ms.
* "3D 8D Preset" mode auto-loads tuned values (rotate 10 RPM, stereo
  width 0, head sway on, ambience 12 %, spatial mix 85 %) for the
  classic YouTube-style 8D listening experience.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
DSP PIPELINE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Per-slot pipeline order, applied independently to capture (server)
and playback (local) so each side keeps its own filter state:

    decoder (FFmpeg, libavfilter pitch / speed)
        v
    sb ring (capture + playback)
        v
    Paulstretch (streaming, dual-instance, optional)
        v
    EQ 16-band (master Enable EQ + per-band gain)
        v
    Spatial: Off / L-R Pan / HRTF dual virtual speaker
        v
    Wet/dry crossfade with raw stereo (Spatial mix)
        v
    Reverb (Ambience + FxPanel reverb summed; END of pipeline)
        v
    Soft limiter (tanh past 0.95)
        v
    int16 clamp + per-slot volume scaling
        v
    PeakMeter limit (legacy earrape protection)
        v
    output

* FxPanel reverb relocated from libavfilter at the decoder to the
  end of the per-slot DSP chain (when sandbox is active) so it
  sounds like a real "room" effect after spatial / EQ.
* Anti-denormal injection at the top of the per-sample loop keeps
  every IIR cascade above the float subnormal floor - eliminates
  the robotic background hiss that used to surface on 8D mode over
  silent intros.
* Soft limiter tightened to T=0.95 with a strict bound at +/-1.0.

NOTABLE BUG FIXES SINCE 1.0.x
---------------------------------------------------------------------
* Theme leak: opening the soundboard no longer wipes TS3's host
  qApp stylesheet - the theme is bracketed and append-merged.
* Drag-swap of grid cells no longer crashes (deferred via QTimer
  singleShot so the drop event finishes before the model rebuild).
* Sound-button cells respect their custom colour + theme palette
  without flipping to opaque black on Qt 5.15.2 (QColor() invalid
  alpha=255 trap).
* HSL adjustL clamps h>=0 so achromatic / grey pickers don't
  collapse the derived palette to pure black.
* Per-deep-widget refreshTheme on FxPanel + VolumeControl + Channel
  so QSS cascade misses don't leave sliders un-themed.
* Reset Channels properly de-greens the link / sync toggle buttons
  the first time (refreshTheme now always runs after setLinked /
  setSync, even when the value already matches).
* PeakMeter no longer compresses every transient on DSP-active
  slots - the limiter still protects against earrape but the main
  quality drop reported as "compressed YouTube" is gone.
* Robotic transient on toggling "Mute on my client" (or any
  capture / playback enable change) eliminated by giving each path
  its own EQ + Positional + Reverb state.
* Robotic burst on starting a new sound on a sandbox-active slot
  eliminated by resetting per-slot DSP state in playSoundInSlot.
* Robotic burst on activating 3D mode eliminated by snapping
  Positional smoothers to target on reset, resetting PathState on
  spatial-mode transitions, and guarding Reverb buffer realloc
  against same-rate setSampleRate calls.
